Scan QR Codes and Barcodes Instantly

QR & Barcode Scanner, Reader is a fast and easy-to-use Android app for scanning QR codes and barcodes. Simply point your camera at a code to access links, WiFi details, contact information, product codes, and more.

Fast and Accurate Scanning

  • Scan common QR code and barcode formats quickly and accurately.
  • Scan codes using your camera or select an image from your device.
  • Use the flashlight in low-light conditions.
  • Zoom to scan barcodes from a distance.
  • Save and manage your scan history.
  • Scan supported QR codes and barcodes offline.

Create and Customize QR Codes

Create QR codes for:

  • Website and URL
  • WiFi networks
  • Text and SMS
  • Email and phone numbers
  • Contacts
  • Locations
  • Calendar events
  • And more

Customize colors, patterns, eye styles, and logos to create QR codes that match your style. Save and share your QR codes, or display them on your screen so others can scan them instantly.

Scan Product Barcodes

Scan product barcodes to view available product information or search online for more details.

Supported formats include QR Code, Data Matrix, Aztec, UPC, EAN, Code 39, Code 93, Code 128, PDF417, ITF, ISBN, and other common formats.

Permissions

  • Camera: Used to scan QR codes and barcodes.
  • Photos or image access: Used when you select an existing image to scan or save and share generated QR code images, where supported.
  • Flashlight: Used to illuminate codes during camera scanning in dim environments, where supported.
Helpful to know: Product details and online search results may depend on external websites or services. Information may not be available for every barcode.
