Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QR & Barcode Scanner, Reader (the “App”) handles information when you use the App. The App provides QR code and barcode scanning, scan history, and QR code creation features.

1. Information and Content Accessed

Depending on the features you choose to use, the App may access content that you scan, create, select, save, or share. This may include QR code and barcode contents, text, URLs, WiFi details, email addresses, phone numbers, contact details, location information, calendar information, and images selected from your device.

Scan results and generated QR codes may be saved on your device to support scan history and saved-code features, depending on the App version and its implementation.

2. Device Permissions

  • Camera: Allows the App to capture QR codes and barcodes for scanning. Camera access is used for the scanning feature and is not intended for continuous recording.
  • Photos or Image Access: Allows you to select an image containing a code for scanning, or to save and share generated QR code images, where supported.
  • Flashlight: May be used to illuminate a code while scanning with the camera.

You can manage or revoke permissions through your Android device settings. Features that rely on a denied permission may not work.

3. How Information Is Used

Information and device access are used to provide App functionality, including decoding codes, displaying scan results, creating QR codes, maintaining scan history, and enabling saving or sharing.

If you choose to open a link, search for product details, or use another external action, relevant information may be sent to the website or service you select.

4. Third-Party Services and Sharing

The App does not sell your personal information. Depending on the version you use, the App may include third-party services such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other service providers.

These providers may process certain device or usage information under their own privacy policies. Please review the actual SDKs and data practices included in the published App build.

When you choose to share a QR code or open an external link, information is shared with the app, recipient, website, or service involved in that action.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

Scan history and saved QR codes may be stored locally on your device and remain there until you delete them or uninstall the App, depending on the implementation.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that requires action, please contact us so we can review the request.

7. Your Choices

You can deny or revoke device permissions in Android settings, delete saved history or codes using available App controls, and choose whether to open or share scan results. Revoking permissions may limit related features.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
